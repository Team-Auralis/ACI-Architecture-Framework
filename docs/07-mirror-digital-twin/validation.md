# Validation & Drift Correction

A simulation is dangerous if it stops matching reality.

## The Feedback Loop
MIRROR constantly grades its own homework.
1.  **Prediction:** The Twin predicts that a flooded highway will become impassable in 2 hours.
2.  **Reality:** Real-world sensors confirm the highway became impassable in 45 minutes.
3.  **Correction:** The discrepancy triggers a validation error. The underlying mathematical weights (e.g., the assumed water flow rate) are automatically adjusted.

This continuous feedback loop ensures that the longer ORION is deployed, the more terrifyingly accurate its Digital Twin becomes at predicting physical reality.
