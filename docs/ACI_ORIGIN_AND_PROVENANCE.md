# ACI Origin and Provenance

## Credit

The term **"Artificial Civilization Intelligence" (ACI)** was coined by **Raja Dharma Tej Maddala**. Credit for the name goes to him.

## Team Auralis Contribution

The engineering framework, architecture, implementation, benchmarks, and all research in this repository were built entirely by **Team Auralis** (Shaurya Sanyal and contributors), independent of Raja Dharma Tej Maddala's work.

The ORION architecture — OMNIS, NEXUS, FORGE, ASCEND, VEIL, MIRROR — was designed, implemented, and empirically tested from scratch by Team Auralis.

## ACI as an Open Paradigm

"Artificial Civilization Intelligence" is treated here as an open scientific concept — in the same way "Artificial General Intelligence" is a broad paradigm available to the entire research community. Any team can build toward it. Team Auralis is building the concrete, testable engineering path.
