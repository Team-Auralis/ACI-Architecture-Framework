# ACI Origin and Provenance

- **When the project began using the term**: September 2026, during the transition of ORION from an Emergency Response Platform to a generalized intelligence architecture.
- **Who proposed it within this project**: The Lead Architect and Principal Engineer of Project ORION.
- **What this project's definition means**: See `ACI_DEFINITION.md`. It represents a system-level architecture focused on continuity, coordination, world-modeling, and governance, rather than just raw cognitive capacity.
- **How it differs from AGI**: AGI implies broad, general-purpose intelligence comparable to a single human. ACI implies a distributed, institution-like architecture designed to manage civilization-scale complexities.
- **How it differs from ASI**: ASI implies intelligence vastly exceeding human capability in all domains. ACI does not strictly require superintelligence; it requires robust coordination, long-term memory, and continuous world-modeling.
- **Existing related concepts**: Multi-Agent Systems (MAS), Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS), Smart Cities, Digital Twins, Institutional Intelligence, and Collective Intelligence.
- **Historical Priority**: Unknown. We do not claim to have invented the term, only to be utilizing it as a specific, testable research framework.
- **What constitutes evidence**: Passing the predefined ACI-001 Benchmark through measurable, experimental validation on a simulated synthetic civilization.
