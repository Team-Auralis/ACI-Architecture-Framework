# ACI Origin and Provenance

## Historical Priority & Coining of the Term
The conceptual framework of **Artificial Civilization Intelligence (ACI)** was first proposed and developed by **Raja Dharma Tej Maddala**, founder of Raja MagRex AI (incorporated Oct 2025, public recognition March 2026). His foundational work defined ACI as a multi-dimensional cognitive framework that moves beyond single-model systems to mirror how a civilization thinks, coordinating diverse cognitive perspectives (logic, ethics, creativity) at scale.

**Team Auralis acknowledges Raja Dharma Tej Maddala's historical priority in coining and defining the philosophical and conceptual boundaries of ACI.**

## Team Auralis Contribution: The Open Architecture Framework
Building upon the conceptual foundation of ACI, Team Auralis (September 2026) proposes a concrete, testable engineering architecture and benchmark designed to make the ACI paradigm empirically measurable.

While the original concept establishes *what* an ACI is (a civilization-scale, multi-perspective cognitive system), the ORION architecture proposes *how* to build, govern, and evaluate it through specific, decoupled subsystems:

- **OMNIS**: The temporal, causal world-model database.
- **NEXUS**: The multi-agent coordination and dispute resolution fabric.
- **FORGE**: The scientific hypothesis and simulation engine.
- **ASCEND**: The long-horizon planner.

## The ACI-001 Benchmark
To ensure ACI remains a rigorous scientific pursuit rather than a marketing buzzword, Team Auralis introduced the **ACI-001 Benchmark**. 

**What constitutes evidence of ACI?**
A system cannot be claimed as an ACI simply because it uses multiple agents. Under the Auralis framework, evidence requires passing the ACI-001 Benchmark through measurable, experimental validation on a simulated synthetic civilization (the **MIRROR** environment), demonstrating multi-decade planning, scientific discovery, and strict policy governance without human intervention.
