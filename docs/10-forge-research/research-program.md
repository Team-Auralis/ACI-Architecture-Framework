# The Research Program

ORION pushes the boundaries of distributed systems. FORGE formalizes the study of these edge cases.

## Core Research Vectors
1.  **Delay-Tolerant Cryptography:** How to safely rotate and verify civilian identity tokens when the user's phone has been offline for 14 days and is communicating via a 5th-hop BLE mesh.
2.  **Adversarial AI Defense (Bounded Autonomy):** Mathematically proving that an LLM cannot manipulate the JSON schema of a payload to bypass the OPA policy firewall.
3.  **Spatial Conflict Resolution:** How to resolve database merge conflicts (CRDTs) when two disconnected edge nodes both assign the same physical responder to two different locations, and then reconnect to the cloud simultaneously.
