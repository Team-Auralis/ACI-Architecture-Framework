# Civilian Vision

Modern smartphones are powerful radios crippled by their reliance on centralized towers.

The vision for HAVEN is to unlock the latent hardware capabilities of civilian devices, transforming every smartphone in a disaster zone into a node within a self-healing emergency network. 

If a hurricane destroys the cellular grid, civilians should not be reduced to holding signs on rooftops. HAVEN ensures that simply having a charged phone makes you part of the rescue operation. By gossiping encrypted packets device-to-device, a trapped civilian's SOS can physically travel miles across a city in the pockets of other survivors until it reaches an ORION gateway.
