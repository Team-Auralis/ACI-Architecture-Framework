# AI Vision

The vision for SENTIENCE is extreme cognitive power with heavily bounded physical reach.

## The Cognitive Bottleneck
In a planetary-scale disaster, data is not the problem; attention is. A command center will receive 50,000 SOS signals, 10,000 sensor alarms, and 500 drone video feeds simultaneously. A human operator cannot triage this.

## The SENTIENCE Approach
SENTIENCE acts as the ultimate filter. It is an ensemble of specialized, narrow AI models and large reasoning engines working in concert to make sense of chaos.
*   It clusters 5,000 overlapping SOS signals into a single "Incident Zone."
*   It analyzes wind telemetry to predict where a wildfire will be in 2 hours.
*   It reads chaotic, panicked civilian text messages and structures them into standardized JSON formats.

However, the vision strictly rejects the "Omnipotent AI" trope. ORION does not allow an AI to unilaterally dispatch helicopters or control physical infrastructure. It is a highly advanced **Decision Support System**, not a dictator.
