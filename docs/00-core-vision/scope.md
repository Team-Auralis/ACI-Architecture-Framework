# Scope Boundaries

To prevent ORION from losing its focus, the following boundaries are strictly enforced.

## In Scope
ORION documentation and architecture covers:
* Resilient communications (civilian and terrestrial)
* Emergency coordination
* Satellite/NTN communication concepts
* Mesh and edge networking concepts
* Identity architecture, Authentication, Authorization, Zero-trust principles
* Policy enforcement & Event-driven architecture
* Application state & Data architecture
* AI orchestration, AI decision support, Human-in-the-loop systems
* Digital twins & Simulation
* Cloud infrastructure & Edge computing
* Distributed systems & Observability
* Resilience, Disaster recovery
* Research & Controlled cybersecurity testing
* Architecture governance

## Explicitly OUT of Scope
ORION will **NEVER** create operational designs for:
* Autonomous weapons
* Weapons launch systems
* Targeting systems
* Missile control
* Autonomous force deployment
* Harmful operational military control systems

ORION is strictly focused on **communications, resilience, infrastructure coordination, simulation, security, and decision support**.
