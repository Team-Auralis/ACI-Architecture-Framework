# Stakeholders

ORION is designed to serve multiple distinct groups.

## 1. Civilians (HAVEN)
Individuals caught in emergency situations requiring immediate assistance. They interact with ORION via standard smartphones using mesh networks and eventual satellite links to broadcast SOS signals.

## 2. Emergency Responders
The primary operators of the system in the field. They utilize edge nodes to coordinate rescue efforts, relying on ORION's disconnected-edge capabilities to maintain local comms when cut off from command.

## 3. Command Centers & Infrastructure Operators
The strategic oversight layer. They utilize the Next.js Dashboards and Digital Twins (MIRROR) to visualize the planetary or regional state of the mesh, authorizing high-level AI recommendations.

## 4. Architecture & Engineering (Team Auralis)
The developers building the system. They are bound by the governance rules, ensuring the system remains highly decoupled and resilient.
