# ACI vs AGI vs ASI

## Definitions

- **ANI (Artificial Narrow Intelligence)**: Intelligence focused on a specific task (e.g., facial recognition).
- **AGI (Artificial General Intelligence)**: Broad, general-purpose intelligence approximately comparable to humans across many intellectual domains.
- **ASI (Artificial Superintelligence)**: General intelligence substantially exceeding humans across essentially all intellectual domains.
- **ACI (Artificial Civilization Intelligence)**: A system-level architecture focused on persistent civilization-scale modeling, coordination, long-horizon planning, scientific progress, real-world interaction, institutional memory, and governance.

## The Paradigm Shift: Why ACI Transcends ASI

While ASI focuses exclusively on the *cognitive depth* of a single model, ACI focuses on the *architectural breadth* of an entire intelligent ecosystem. 

**ACI does not compete with ASI; it subsumes it.**

If an ASI is developed, it would simply become one highly capable agent within the ACI's **NEXUS** fabric. The ACI architecture provides the memory, the world model, the safety constraints, and the real-world actuation that an ASI lacks. 

An ACI system might utilize one ASI, multiple AGI models, or thousands of ANI models. It is the **structured coordination, governance, and accumulated state** that makes ACI the ultimate framework for planetary-scale intelligence.
