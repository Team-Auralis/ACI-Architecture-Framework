# The Intelligence Taxonomy: AI, AGI, ASI, and ACI

To understand ACI, we must decouple the measurement of **Cognitive Depth** from **Systemic Architecture**. 

The traditional AI roadmap (AI → AGI → ASI) measures only cognitive depth. It assumes that increasing the IQ of a single model solves all problems. This is structurally false.

### 1. AI (Artificial Narrow Intelligence)
- **Definition:** The computational automation of specific, bounded tasks (e.g., facial recognition, protein folding).
- **Architecture:** Stateless, singular.
- **Limitation:** Fails completely outside its training distribution.

### 2. AGI (Artificial General Intelligence)
- **Definition:** A single cognitive node capable of executing any intellectual task a human can.
- **Architecture:** General-purpose, singular.
- **Limitation:** Lacks persistent environmental state. If you wipe its context window, it loses all continuity. It is an oracle, not an institution.

### 3. ASI (Artificial Superintelligence)
- **Definition:** A single cognitive node whose capability vastly exceeds human bounds in all domains.
- **Architecture:** Hyper-capable, singular.
- **Limitation:** A single point of failure. An ASI without governance, multi-agent error correction, or a persistent world model is an untethered god in a box. It cannot inherently execute long-term physical logistics without human-designed infrastructure.

### 4. ACI (Artificial Civilization Intelligence)
- **Definition:** The distributed, governed architecture required to manage planetary-scale intelligence.
- **Architecture:** Multi-nodal, stateful, persistent, governed.
- **Advantage:** ACI subsumes AI, AGI, and ASI. An ACI can plug a superintelligent ASI into its NEXUS fabric as a compute node. 

## The Ultimate Distinction
**ASI answers the question:** *"What is the most intelligent single entity possible?"*
**ACI answers the question:** *"How do you safely operate a planetary civilization using intelligence?"*

Humanity did not conquer the planet because a single human achieved a 10,000 IQ. We conquered the planet through language, institutions, shared world-models, science, and governance. **Civilization itself is the ultimate intelligence algorithm.** 

ACI is the digital embodiment of that algorithm.
