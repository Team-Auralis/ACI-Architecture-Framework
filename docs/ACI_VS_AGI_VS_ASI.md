# ACI vs AGI vs ASI

## Definitions

- **ANI (Artificial Narrow Intelligence)**: Narrow intelligence focused on a specific task (e.g., playing chess, facial recognition).
- **AGI (Artificial General Intelligence)**: Broad, general-purpose intelligence approximately comparable to humans across many intellectual domains.
- **ASI (Artificial Superintelligence)**: General intelligence substantially exceeding humans across essentially all intellectual domains.
- **ACI (Artificial Civilization Intelligence)**: A proposed system-level architecture focused on persistent civilization-scale modeling, coordination, long-horizon planning, scientific progress, real-world interaction, institutional memory, and governance.

## Key Distinctions

ACI does NOT necessarily mean "smarter than ASI" or even "smarter than AGI."

ACI describes a different dimension of intelligence scaling:
**SCALE + CONTINUITY + COORDINATION + WORLD MODEL + INSTITUTIONAL MEMORY + ACTION + GOVERNANCE**

An ACI system might utilize multiple AGI models, or thousands of ANI models, coordinated through a structured governance and world-model framework.
