# ACI: The Definitive Framework

## Core Definition
**Artificial Civilization Intelligence (ACI)** is not a measure of a single model's cognitive capacity. It is the **structural operating system** required to sustain, coordinate, and govern intelligence at a planetary scale.

If Artificial Superintelligence (ASI) is a hyper-advanced processor, **ACI is the motherboard, the memory bus, the operating system, and the security kernel combined.**

ACI is formally defined as an intelligence architecture that satisfies seven structural axioms:

1. **Graph-Theoretic State (OMNIS):** Must maintain a continuous, causal, and updateable world-model rather than relying on static training weights.
2. **Heterogeneous Coordination (NEXUS):** Must coordinate specialized, adversarial agents (including humans, AI, AGI, or ASI) to resolve conflict and aggregate truth.
3. **Temporal Continuity (ASCEND):** Must be capable of planning, executing, measuring error, and replanning over multi-year or multi-decade horizons.
4. **Empirical Verification (FORGE):** Must autonomously test hypotheses against simulated or physical reality before updating its core knowledge graph.
5. **Physical Embeddedness (ATLAS/PHOENIX):** Must interface directly with physical infrastructure (energy, logistics, communications).
6. **Strict Governance (VEIL):** Must operate under cryptographic authority boundaries, enforcing Zero-Trust safety across all nodes.
7. **Antifragility:** Must survive the destruction or degradation of individual cognitive nodes without catastrophic failure of the civilization model.

## The ORION Proof
Project ORION is being constructed as the first concrete, empirical proof of the ACI framework. Through the ACI-001 Benchmark, ORION aims to prove that distributed, governed coordination (ACI) yields higher civilization resilience and scientific output than isolated super-cognition (ASI).
