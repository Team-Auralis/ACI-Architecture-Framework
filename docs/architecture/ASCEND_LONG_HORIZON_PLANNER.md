# ASCEND Long-Horizon Planning

ASCEND handles planning across temporal scales: days, months, years, and decades. 

## Planner Capabilities
- Objectives & Constraints
- Milestones & Dependencies
- Resource budgets
- Risk & Uncertainty quantification
- Alternative strategies
- Replanning & Failure recovery
- Progress measurement

## Example Objective
**"Reduce water stress by 30% within 10 years."**
1. 10-year objective
2. 5-year objectives
3. 3-year objectives
4. Annual milestones
5. Quarterly actions
6. Measurable outcomes

When reality differs from predictions:
`OBSERVE → MEASURE ERROR → UPDATE MODEL → REPLAN`

*Note: Generating a long text plan is not long-horizon planning. ASCEND must track actual outcomes and correct course.*
