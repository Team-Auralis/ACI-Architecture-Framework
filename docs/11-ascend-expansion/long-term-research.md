# Long-Term Research Vectors

Looking 10 to 20 years ahead.

## 1. AI Treaty Negotiation
When two sovereign AI orchestrators (e.g., a US AI and a European AI) are managing a joint disaster response, how do they computationally negotiate resource allocation (e.g., sharing satellite bandwidth) at machine speed without human intervention?

## 2. Quantum-Secure Routing
As quantum computing threatens standard public-key cryptography (RSA/ECC), how do we live-migrate a planetary-scale mesh of 100 million devices to post-quantum cryptographic algorithms (e.g., Kyber) without incurring network downtime?

## 3. Swarm Robotics at the Edge
Integrating swarms of thousands of cheap, disposable autonomous drones that utilize the ORION BLE mesh to coordinate search patterns over a destroyed city, dynamically electing their own leader nodes if the primary command drone is destroyed.
