## The Global Nervous System
When multiple National ORION meshes federate together, backed by global LEO satellite constellations (Starlink/AST SpaceMobile) and integrated with global weather models.
*   An earthquake in Japan triggers sensors.
*   The Japanese ORION mesh processes the telemetry.
*   **SENTIENCE AI** predicts a tsunami trajectory.
*   The Japanese mesh federates the prediction over the NATS Gateway to the United States ORION mesh.
*   The US mesh automatically triggers evacuation sirens in Hawaii.

The total latency from the sensor trigger in Japan to the siren in Hawaii is less than 500 milliseconds. This is the definition of a planetary reflex.
