    Academic Literature Verification: Grounding ACI Research in Peer-Reviewed Science¶

    Author: Shaurya Sanyal (Team Auralis) · Origin Provenance: The term "ACI" was coined by Raja Dharma Tej Maddala.
    Status: Peer-Reviewed Cross-Verification Ledger
    Indexation Basis: Google Scholar, arXiv, Nature, Transactions of the ACL (TACL), and ICLR
    Date: November 2026

    1. Executive Summary¶

    To ensure maximum academic rigor, this report cross-references every foundational theoretical and empirical claim in the ACI Architecture Framework against peer-reviewed literature indexed on Google Scholar, Nature, and major computer science conferences.

    Our research is grounded in four independently validated pillars of modern AI science: 1. The Softmax Attention Dilution Bottleneck: Verified by Lost in the Middle (Liu et al., Stanford/Berkeley, TACL 2024) and recent theoretical studies on zero-sum attention normalization. 2. Error Gravitation & Limits of Unassisted Self-Correction: Verified by Google DeepMind's seminal study Large Language Models Cannot Self-Correct Reasoning Yet (Huang, Chen, Zhou et al., ICLR 2024). 3. Evolutionary Program Search for Scientific Discovery: Verified by Google DeepMind's FunSearch (Romera-Paredes et al., Nature 2024) and Google Research's ERA (Empirical Research Assistant) (Aygün et al., arXiv:2509.06503, 2025). 4. Adaptive Tiered Working Memory in Agents: Grounded in Megiddo & Modha's Adaptive Replacement Cache (IBM Research, FAST 2003) and MemGPT (Packer et al., 2023).

    The Mechanism of Attention Degradation in Scientific Code Search¶

    In both FunSearch (Nature 2024) and ERA (arXiv:2509.06503, 2025), candidate code is generated by prompting an LLM with past programs and evaluation scores.

    However, in complex empirical domains (e.g. quantum chemical kinetics, tokamak magnetohydrodynamics, sub-2nm lithography, and high-voltage grid dispatch): 1. Raw Log Accumulation: Compilers, sandboxes, and physics simulators produce verbose diagnostic outputs (AddressSanitizer traces, matrix singularity logs, SCADA alarms). 2. Context Dilution: As shown by Liu et al. (Lost in the Middle), when this unmanaged feedback accumulates in the prompt history, attention to foundational constraints (conservation laws, convergence bounds, forbidden library calls) degrades asymptotically toward zero: $\(\lim_{d \to \infty} A_{i, \text{inv}} = \mathcal{O}\left( \frac{L_u}{\sum_{k=1}^d L(\text{Trace}_k)} \right) \longrightarrow 0\)$ 3. Error Gravitation: As demonstrated by Huang et al. (ICLR 2024), an LLM prompted with multi-line traceback dumps attempts myopic surface-level syntax fixes rather than preserving the mathematical formulation.

    The ACI Generational Context Solution¶

    ACI's Gen-FUTS resolves this by decoupling invariant tenure from ephemeral runtime logs: - Tenured Invariants (Gen-2): Pinned at the top of the context window, exploiting the Primacy Effect proven in Lost in the Middle. - Ephemeral Distillation (Gen-0): Intercepts raw tracebacks (500–2,000 tokens) and distills them into atomic \(\mathcal{O}(1)\) tuples (Status, Score, Delta, RootCause) (<50 tokens). - Working Memory Management (Gen-1): Uses an adaptive replacement attention cache (AWE) to evaporate failed branch hypotheses, guaranteeing a bounded context budget (\(C_{\text{bound}} < \infty\)).
